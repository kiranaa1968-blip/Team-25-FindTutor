**THE PROBLEM**  
There can be difficulty in finding a suitable peer tutor for specific courses since any information about students willing to tutor are usually split across social media platforms and word of mouth, and this can be much more troublesome depending on the courses they need tutoring for. As well, any students who are capable and willing to tutor don’t really have any centralized way to advertise their availability.

**Process: Agile**  
For our project, we feel that it would be best if Agile was to be our SDLC. Why? We are building a project for students to have an easier way in finding a tutor or advertising their tutoring, so it would be best if we were to deliver a smaller version of project, get feedbacks, and adjust to those feedbacks.
